Frequently asked questions

Is FreeCyberCloud really free?

Yes. FreeCyberCloud is completely free — there is no payment, no subscription and no paywall. Create an account and start uploading right away.

How much can I upload?

You can upload multiple files at once, up to 5 MB per file. Your dashboard shows how much storage you are using.

Are my files private?

Yes. Each account can only read and write its own files; security rules on the database block access to other users' data.

Which devices can I use?

FreeCyberCloud works in any modern browser on phones, tablets and computers. Sign in on any device to reach your files.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Everything runs in your browser — just sign in at freecybercloud.netlify.app.
