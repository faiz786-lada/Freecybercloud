Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

FreeCyberCloud (freecybercloud.netlify.app) is a free cloud storage service. This page explains what we store and how it is used.

What we collect

  • Your email address, display name and password (the password is handled by Firebase Authentication and is never visible to us).
  • The files, folders and settings you create inside your account.
  • Basic usage analytics (pages viewed, device and browser type) through Google Analytics for Firebase.

How your data is used

Your data is used only to run the service: sign you in, store your files and show them back to you. We do not sell your data and do not show ads.

Who can see your files

Database security rules allow each signed-in user to read and write only their own data.

Where data is stored

Account and file data is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Realtime Database).

Deleting your data

You can delete any file permanently from the Trash page at any time. To remove your whole account, contact the site owner through the website.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest revision.
